Theory: Value Noise

hash(p) -> pseudo-random number in [0,1) per lattice point noise(p) -> trilinear blend of the 8 surrounding hashes with smooth weights s = f·f·(3 - 2f) Sampled at the 3D surface point, not at UV, so the texture has no seam where u wraps from 1 back to 0.

Theory: fbm (fractal noise)

fbm(p) = Σ amp_i · noise(2^i · p), amp_i = 0.5^(i+1) Each octave doubles the frequency and halves the strength. Used for dough speckles, icing tint and bump height.

Theory: Icing Mask & Drips

g(u) = 0.5 + 0.25·sin(2π·3u) + 0.15·sin(2π·7u+1.3) + 0.1·sin(2π·13u+4) edge(u) = 0.35 - 0.7·g(u) mask = smoothstep(edge-0.03, edge+0.03, sin(2πv)) sin(2πv) is the height on the tube (1 = top). Integer sine frequencies keep the edge periodic in u. Where edge is low the icing drips further down the side.

Theory: Layers & Sprinkles

albedo = mix(dough, icing, mask) albedo = mix(albedo, chocolate, stripe · mask) Sprinkles: split UV into a 60x24 grid, hash each cell, 10% of cells get a rotated capsule with a hashed color. Chocolate: stripe = smoothstep(0.8,0.88, 0.5+0.5·sin(2πw)) w = 14u + 1.5·sin(4πv) + 1.2·fbm(p)

Theory: Displacement (vertex shader)

P' = P + N · thickness · mask · (0.6 + 0.4·fbm) Icing physically sits above the dough. The normal is left unchanged for simplicity, so the bump shading below supplies the detail.

Theory: Bump Mapping

Height h(p) comes from noise. Its screen-space slope: dh/dx = dFdx(h), dh/dy = dFdy(h) N' = normalize( |det|·N - sign(det)·(dh/dx·r1 + dh/dy·r2) ) r1 = cross(dFdy(P), N), r2 = cross(N, dFdx(P)) Derivatives replace a stored normal map or tangents.
